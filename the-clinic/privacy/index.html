Privacy Policy

The Clinic · a Codalix product
Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what The Clinic ("the app") does with information. In short: the app keeps patient records on the clinic's own computer, and Codalix does not receive them.

Who we are

The Clinic is made by Codalix (codalix.org). Contact: support@codalix.org.

What the app works with

The app helps a clinic keep its records. The clinic enters, and the app stores:

  • patient details: name, age, gender, phone number, address and an optional photo,
  • visits and eye examinations, prescriptions and glasses prescriptions,
  • the clinic's daily fees and settings.

This is health information. The clinic is responsible for it and decides how it is used. Codalix does not see it.

What is sent to Codalix

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. It does not send patient information, sign-in details or usage to Codalix. Licenses are activated from a code, without contacting a Codalix server.

Optional features that use the internet

Some features connect to a service that the clinic chooses, only when the clinic turns them on and uses them, for example sending a message or a prescription file by WhatsApp, or keeping a backup copy in a cloud storage folder. These features send only what the clinic chooses to send, to the service the clinic chooses, and that service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Codalix does not receive a copy.

What is stored, and where

Patient records are stored in a database on the clinic's computer. Nothing is stored on a Codalix server.

Back up your data. When the app is installed from the Microsoft Store, removing the app can also remove the data it stored on that computer. Before you uninstall the app or replace the computer, export or back up your records.

Keeping the data safe

The clinic should protect the computer: sign in to Windows with a password, lock the screen when away, encrypt the disk, and keep regular backups in a safe place.

Sharing

We do not sell or share patient information, because we do not receive it.

Not medical advice

The Clinic is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose and does not replace the doctor's judgment.

Your choices

The clinic decides what to enter, which optional features to use and what to keep. Patients who want their information corrected or removed should ask their clinic, which holds the records.

Children

The app is a business tool for clinics. Clinics may enter records of children as part of their patients. The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@codalix.org
